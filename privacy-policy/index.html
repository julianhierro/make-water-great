Privacy policy

Last updated November 6, 2026

This policy explains what Make Great Water LLC (“we” or “us”) does with your information when you use this website or ask us about our water treatment services.

Information we collect

What you give us. When you fill in a form, we collect your name, phone number, email address and ZIP code, along with what you tell us about your water and home. If you become a customer, we also keep your address, your system and service details, your warranty paperwork and your payment records.

What is collected automatically. Like most websites, this site and the services that run it may receive technical details such as your IP address, browser, device and the pages you visit.

How we use it

  • To answer your request, schedule your water test and prepare your quote
  • To install and service your system and handle warranty claims
  • To contact you about your request or service by phone, text or email
  • To keep our records, meet legal obligations, and protect our business and customers
  • To understand and improve our website and services

We won’t send you marketing texts unless you’ve agreed to receive them. You can opt out of marketing messages at any time.

Who we share it with

We don’t sell your personal information. We share it only as needed with:

  • Service providers that help us run the business, such as our website host and our customer management, messaging and payment tools. We use GoHighLevel for customer management and messaging.
  • Equipment distributors and manufacturers, when needed to supply your system or process a warranty claim
  • Licensed plumbers or subcontractors who carry out your installation or service
  • Government agencies, courts or others when the law requires it, or to protect our legal rights
  • A buyer or successor, if our business is sold or reorganized

Cookies and outside services

We don’t currently use advertising or analytics cookies on this site. Our pages load fonts from Google Fonts and some images from Unsplash, so those services may receive your IP address and browser details when you visit. Their own privacy policies apply to that information.

How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as we need it for the purposes above. Customer and warranty records may be kept for as long as your warranty lasts and as required for tax, legal and accounting purposes.

How we protect it

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information, but no website or storage system is completely secure, so we can’t guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You can ask us to give you a copy of your personal information, or to correct or delete it. We may need to confirm your identity first, and we may keep information the law requires us to retain. To make a request, contact us.

Children

Our services are for adults, and we don’t knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we’ll delete it.

Links to other sites

Our site may link to websites we don’t control. This policy doesn’t cover them.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we’ll post the new version here and update the date at the top.
