Warranty

Make Water Great LLC Warranty Statement

Section 1: Make Water Great LLC Lifetime Warranty

At Make Water Great LLC, we take pride in the quality and craftsmanship of our water treatment systems. The following limited warranties apply to the original purchaser of each system:

Coverage

  • Lifetime Warranty – System Dry Parts
    Covers all mechanical and non-consumable components that do not come into direct contact with water.
  • 5-Year Warranty – Control Valve, Circuit Board, and Filtration Media
    Protects against defects in materials and workmanship under normal operating conditions.
  • 1-Year Warranty – Labor
    Covers labor-related issues arising from the original installation for a period of one year from the date of purchase.

Warranty Conditions & Limitations

  • Coverage is valid only when the system is installed, operated, and maintained according to Make Water Great LLC guidelines.
  • The warranty excludes damages caused by improper installation, misuse, neglect, unauthorized modifications, or environmental factors such as flooding, freezing, or excessive contaminant levels beyond system specifications.
  • Replacement parts or services rendered under this warranty do not extend or reset the original warranty period.
  • This warranty is the exclusive remedy and supersedes all other warranties—express or implied—including warranties of merchantability and fitness for a particular purpose.
  • Make Water Great LLC is only responsible for damages or failures resulting from workmanship-related issues. Make Water Great LLC is not responsible for any damages caused by manufacturer defects.
  • Make Water Great LLC’s total liability is strictly limited to the original purchase price of the system. We are not liable for any incidental, consequential, or indirect damages.

To file a claim, please contact Make Water Great LLC Customer Support and provide proof of purchase along with installation documentation.

Exclusions

This warranty does not apply to damages caused by:

  • Misuse, abuse, neglect, or accidents
  • Improper installation or unauthorized repairs

  • Water conditions that foul filters, resins, or membranes

To process a claim, the following must be provided:

  • Valid proof of purchase
  • Installation documentation
  • Return authorization number issued by Make Water Great LLC
