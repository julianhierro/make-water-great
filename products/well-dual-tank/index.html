Dual-Tank Well System

One tank for iron and sulfur, one for hardness.

For private wells

Priced after your free water test

Orange stains, a metal taste and a rotten egg smell all come from things a normal softener can’t deal with for long. The first tank uses oxidizing media to catch iron, manganese and hydrogen sulfide. The second softens what’s left.

Best for: Homes on a private well with staining, odor or high hardness.

Book a free water test

What it does

  • Iron and manganese
  • Hydrogen sulfide (rotten egg smell)
  • Hardness

What it won’t do

Bacteria or nitrates. Have a lab test your well first, and add UV if the results call for it.
