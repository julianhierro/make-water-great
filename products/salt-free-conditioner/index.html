Salt-Free Conditioner

Helps limit scale, with no salt to buy or brine tank to fit.

Priced after your free water test

A conditioner changes how the minerals behave so they’re less likely to stick to pipes and appliances. It doesn’t remove them, so it isn’t the same as softening. You won’t get the slippery soft water feel, but there’s no salt to top up and no brine tank.

Best for: Homes that want less scale and don’t want salt.

Book a free water test

What it does

  • Reduces new scale forming

What it won’t do

Softening. The minerals stay in the water, so expect some spotting and no change in how soap lathers.
