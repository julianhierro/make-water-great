Dual-Tank City Water System

Softening and carbon filtration in separate tanks.

Priced after your free water test

Keeping softening and carbon in their own tanks lets each one do its job and be serviced separately. It takes more floor space than a single tank, so it suits homes with room in the garage or utility area.

Best for: City water homes with the space for two tanks.

Book a free water test

What it does

  • Hardness (calcium and magnesium)
  • Chlorine taste and smell

What it won’t do

Lead, nitrates or bacteria. It also needs more room than a single-tank system.
