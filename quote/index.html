Book your free water test

Takes about two minutes to fill in. We’ll get in touch to pick a time.

What’s bothering you?

We only use your details to contact you about your water, and we never sell them. See our privacy policy.

What happens next

  1. We test at your tap

    Hardness, chlorine, iron, pH and dissolved solids, measured in front of you. It takes about fifteen minutes and you keep the numbers.

  2. We explain what the numbers mean

    What’s causing the problem you called about, what it’s doing to your plumbing, and what happens if you leave it alone. Sometimes the honest answer is that you don’t need much.

  3. You get one written quote

    The system sized for your house and the price to install it. You can take it away, think about it and compare it with anyone else’s.

  4. We install it and check our work

    A clean install, a walkthrough of how to run it, and a retest so you can see the before and after numbers side by side.
